6628	5. What the KV cache actually stores
15914	In a causal model, appending a new token does not change old positions' representations at any layer: old positions cannot see the future. Their projected K and V can therefore be saved, while new positions compute only their own Q, K and V and read the historical cache. It caches intermediate tensors for that layer, prefix and model parameters, not an input-independent knowledge base.
22034	The mask for each layer's new chunk is not a simple C×C triangle, but C×(P+C): row i in the new chunk permits keys
10074	. An unaligned "upper-left triangle" hides old prefix positions that new tokens should see. Full-sequence, token-by-token and arbitrary-chunk inference should produce the same logits within tolerance.
16969	The experiment caches rotated K and originally projected V. Other architectures may cache different representations, so this assumption is not universal. Changes in model weights, LoRA adapters, template prefixes or position policies may invalidate existing caches; a cache hit requires matching computational state, not merely shared words in strings.

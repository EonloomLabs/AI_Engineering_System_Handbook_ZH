10522	Three different kinds of conclusion
11638	Fact: in the source, a certain function handles the post-execution hook after the handler returns. Inference: a refusal at that point cannot stop an external action that has already happened. Recommendation: a sensitive operation should be checked before execution, and an unknown result should be given a verification or idempotent path. The three have to be kept apart; a recommendation must not be written up as a guarantee the product already provides.
14936	How to use the graph when making a change
12484	Change a tool schema, and argument assembly, hook rewriting, authorization binding, execution and filling the result back in all need regression. Change compaction, and the key constraints, the historical versions, the calls in flight and recovery need regression. Change memory, and extraction, leases, artifact verification, source withdrawal and expiry at the point of use need regression. The graph helps to settle the scope of the impact; it does not require every small change to rerun every test in the system.
10692	A design that is not worth doing has to be removable too
10482	A mechanism with no matching failure mode and no gain on the evaluation, yet with added calls, state and maintenance cost, should not be kept because it looks complete. Advanced engineering judgment is knowing when a complex mechanism is needed, and equally when simple direct execution is better.

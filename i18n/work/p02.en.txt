A full model, though, may have other position-dependent mechanisms, sliding-window rules, special positions or a scaling strategy; and an already-rotated cache cannot be transformed consistently by changing one position counter. The mathematical shift property is not in itself a safety proof for any given cache-truncation implementation.
28115	Follow-up: how is long-context extension done?
17967	Common approaches include position interpolation, frequency scaling, adjusting particular frequency bands, and continued training on long sequences. They try to bring position patterns beyond the original training length closer to a learnable range while keeping local distinctions. Local precision and long-range coverage may have to be traded off, so real tasks such as retrieval, combining across segments and locating code have to be tested — not just "whether the interface accepts more tokens".
27813	Follow-up: why is it usually Q/K that are rotated rather than V?
27335	This design puts the relative-position signal mainly into the computation of the read weights, while␣
11766	␣still supplies the content being mixed. Other architectures may take other approaches; this implementation choice must not be inflated into a theorem that every model is mathematically obliged to follow. When reading source code, check whether the cache holds K before or after rotation, and whether reuse rotates it again.

20465	Ordinary deployed generation uses fixed parameters: it computes a conditional distribution and chooses a token, with no loss to compute and no weights to update.

29084	What to validate in order to know the model has not peeked
15769	Fix a prefix and change a later token: the logits at the prefix positions should not change; running the same sample on its own and putting it into a separate batch should give the same result; swapping the order of the batch should swap the results correspondingly. These check causality and sample isolation; they do not assess semantic ability.
20992	This model's negative control deliberately turns the causal mask off, to confirm that the test for "a change in the future does not affect the prefix" catches the error. A correct implementation passing is not enough on its own: it also has to be known that the test fails when it meets a broken implementation.
29099	The validation set, overfitting and the limits of this experiment
21816	Formal training should have an isolated validation task, control of data leakage, evaluation on the target domain and reproducible data processing. The worked model uses two sentences over and over and has no credible generalization score. This book does not dress up the continuation accuracy on those same two sentences as a validation-set success rate.
27857	Follow-up: how exactly is the parameter W "taught" by language data?
10350	One sentence forms several real prefixes and next-token labels. The current W affects the probabilities in the forward pass, cross entropy scores the probability of the label, the backward chain rule computes the local effect of every cell of W on this loss, and the optimizer updates step by step according to those effects. No single step writes the meaning of a word directly into a column of labels.
21865	When the probability of the correct item rises from 0.25 to 0.5, does the negative-log loss rise or fall?
10620	It falls, from about 1.3863 to 0.6931. Natural logarithms are used here.
11507	Why might resuming training need the optimizer state as well, and not just the weights?
6948	Optimizers such as Adam store smoothed history, and the step count and the learning-rate state also affect later updates. Restoring the weights alone does not necessarily continue the same training trajectory.

15145	Could input_ids and targets be made exactly the same, with the shift removed?
28396	That would mainly train "predict the current token from itself", not the next-token conditional probability this book is about. The relationship between the input position, the visible prefix and the target position has to be explicit; the shift is usually done only once.
24982	If two micro-batches have 2 and 8 valid tokens respectively, does averaging the two micro-batches' average losses equal the average over all 10 questions?
10467	Generally not. The correct way is to add the two batches' valid loss sums and divide by 10; the two batch averages should be weighted by 2/10 and 8/10. An equal-weight average gives each token of the shorter micro-batch a larger weight.
12384	Does saving the model parameters make it possible to resume AdamW training exactly?
28279	Usually not. The optimizer's m and v, the step count, the scheduler, any necessary random state, the data progress and so on also have to be saved. Only loading for inference can do without this training state.
25535	If the training-set loss falls and the same two sentences can be continued, does that prove a deployment to other sentences will also succeed?
10989	No. This experiment has neither training coverage of language in general nor an independent evaluation; it serves to verify the computation and the file life cycle, not to prove generalization.
11465	To save memory, wrapping the real training forward pass in no_grad and then calling loss.backward() on the loss — can all the parameters still be learned normally?
28285	Usually not; the computational graph needed has been disabled and the gradient path is broken. Saving training VRAM should use explicit methods such as mixed precision, checkpointing or sharding, with the gradients verified, rather than simply turning off the differentiation that is needed.

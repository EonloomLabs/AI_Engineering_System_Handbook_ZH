29010	Pretraining does not feed raw web pages straight into a GPU. 18259	When business data is used to fine-tune a model, permission and deletion responsibilities extend from the original data to training artifacts. 6276	2. The data flow of one training step
6939	AdamW maintains the first and second moments of gradients and uses them after bias correction to normalize step sizes. Decoupled weight decay applies shrinkage directly in parameter updates; it should not simply be equated with applying L2 penalties uniformly to all parameters. Implementations specify which parameters decay, when accumulation occurs and how the learning rate is scheduled. Reproducing an experiment requires saving these configurations, not only a learning-rate number.
21488	Gradient accumulation combines contributions from several microbatches into one optimizer update. Equivalence depends on loss normalization, stochastic operators and how schedule steps are counted. With different numbers of valid tokens in long and short sequences, a simple mean of microbatch means may differ from the mean over all valid tokens. State whether the objective weights samples or tokens equally before designing accumulation logic.
6418	3. Why VRAM is not just parameter count times 2 bytes
25461	Training may simultaneously hold model weights, gradients, optimizer moments, master-weight copies, intermediate activations, temporary operator buffers and communication buffers. Some common mixed-precision Adam configurations approach 16 bytes of model state per parameter, but this is not a universal constant: FP32 master weights, gradient dtype, sharding and quantized optimizers all change it.

10019	. Judging trainability only from "7B×2 bytes=14GB" is wrong; at most, this estimates weight storage for a particular dtype. Longer sequences also increase activation costs even if model parameters stay unchanged.

6533	4. How distributed training divides work
20043	Data parallelism lets devices process different samples, then synchronize gradients; tensor parallelism splits a layer's matrix computations and requires communication within that layer; pipeline parallelism splits layers, with microbatches flowing across stages and creating pipeline bubbles; expert parallelism assigns MoE experts to devices, with routing moving tokens between devices. They address different constraints and cannot be summarized as "More GPUs make training linearly faster".
28179	Before choosing parallelism, determine whether the bottleneck is fitting on one GPU, matrix computation or the inter-device network. Splitting a large matrix too finely may increase the communication share; uneven pipeline stages let the slowest stage limit the whole; ignoring expert load distribution leaves popular experts queuing. Lesson 59 continues these trade-offs through roofline and bandwidth.
13786	Sharding optimizer state, gradients and parameters reduces what one device holds but introduces collection and communication. Checkpoint recovery also needs optimizer state, random state, data position and schedule counters; otherwise resuming from the same weights need not reproduce the same training trajectory. Production recovery seeks explicit recovery semantics, without requiring bitwise identity on every kind of hardware.

6050	1. How pretrained capabilities become assistant behavior
29011	Pretraining optimizes a text distribution; SFT uses demonstrations to reinforce answer forms and task strategies under particular conditions. High-quality traces of "request → answer → tool result → continued action" let the model learn these patterns, but learning the format does not make every call valid. Schema validation, permissions and completion checks remain in the execution layer.
8806	SFT samples involve more than final text: task distributions, correct tool calls and recovery, and whether the model may see verification feedback all matter. Removing every failed trace may teach only the smooth path; indiscriminately adding failures may copy errors. Data design should specify which actions merit imitation and which content is only environment feedback.
25494	Optimizing only the final answer may leave a dangerous intermediate call in a long trace unconstrained directly. Conversely, mechanically rewarding every step's form can encourage format compliance without task progress. Distinguish objective levels and observable feedback instead of simply seeking longer reasoning.
6269	2. Rewards in RLHF are not factual truth
13523	A typical preference process compares answers, trains a reward model, then optimizes the policy to increase reward while constraining deviation from a reference policy. Preferences may combine helpfulness, style, instruction following, safety and other dimensions; one scalar cannot be assumed to represent every business goal perfectly.
21667	The model may exploit reward-model weaknesses, such as preferences for verbosity, a certain tone or superficial completeness. Seeing "More confident answers" should not imply "More correct facts". Engineering needs externally verifiable outcomes such as compilation, final database state or evidence support rates, not only another model's satisfaction.
18174	Policy updates in reinforcement learning do not mean parameters update instantly during every user chat. Products may have separate data and training pipelines; correcting an answer in the current session changes context directly, while whether it enters later training is a separate question.

6653	5. The boundaries of process supervision, outcome supervision and search
24455	Outcome supervision is inexpensive and direct but struggles to identify the first error in long reasoning; process supervision is finer-grained but needs reliable step labels and may reward steps tailored to a grading format. Multipath sampling, self-consistency voting and tree search spend extra inference-time compute exploring candidates; they do not inherently add base-model knowledge.
25010	If candidates share a false premise, voting only amplifies it. If a verifier prefers fluent language, search may more consistently find wrong but pleasing answers. 17269	For coding tasks, having the model run a relevant test is often more verifiable than generating three long analyses without evidence. Deeper analysis can still be highly valuable for risky cross-module changes.

14061	Distinguish three failures: lacking relevant knowledge, being unable to reason under given conditions, and being unable to execute reliably. 6534	4. Paged KV caching addresses allocation and sharing
28684	Long requests vary in length; reserving contiguous maximum KV space per request wastes capacity and fragments memory. PagedAttention maps logical token positions to fixed-size KV blocks that may be physically noncontiguous. Shared prefixes can reference shared blocks, with suitable sharing/copying rules when writes diverge.
16034	Smaller blocks increase metadata, addressing and management costs; larger blocks increase internal fragmentation in the final block. Cache compression and quantization are separate techniques; paging alone does not automatically reduce K/V bytes per valid token. Sharing a physical prefix does not remove tenant isolation or cache-side-channel concerns.
20683	Serving schedulers must know how much KV capacity can be allocated. Admitting too many long requests may cause preemption, swapping or failures; RPM limits alone cannot reflect this pressure. In-flight tokens, requests and output limits can be constrained separately, with service objectives considered in scheduling.
6624	5. Continuous batching and fairness
28910	Static batching waits for a group of requests to finish together, preventing timely admission of new requests; continuous batching adds or removes requests at iteration boundaries to improve device utilization. But a long prefill can block short requests already decoding and affect per-token latency. Chunking prefill, separating stages or designing fair scheduling each adds complexity and hardware conditions.
19395	Keep metrics separate: TTFT measures first response, TPOT measures intervals between output tokens, and end-to-end time measures the user's task; multitenancy also needs tail latency. Raising batch size for throughput may hurt interaction, while running one request at a time for minimal latency may reduce utilization. An optimum exists only relative to the workload and SLO.

21645	A model's internal KV cache is inference state; a service prefix cache may reuse prefix computation across requests; the billing definition of a cache hit is a product contract again.

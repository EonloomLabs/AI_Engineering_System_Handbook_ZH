10484	The seven-step process, and what failure at each step costs
28194	Choose the boundary.</strong> Compact when the tool calls have finished and the goal of the stage is clear, if you can. If an action is still running/
14015	, its state has to be carried over explicitly and must not vanish.
13653	Freeze a snapshot.</strong> Record the history version v and the current task version. If new messages arrive during compaction, either queue them or merge them again before the switch; otherwise a new constraint from the user gets overwritten.
19764	Extract the key objects.</strong> The goal, the negative constraints, the verified facts, the citations of evidence, the completed actions, the open questions and the next step. The key objects can come from structured state; they need not rest entirely on the model's recollection.
22809	Generate the candidate.</strong> Require that unconfirmed hypotheses are not promoted into facts, and that sources and versions are kept. With a dedicated API, follow its input/output state contract.
21494	Check the invariants.</strong> Are the key IDs and constraints present, is an unknown action still unknown, are the evidence versions consistent, has a negation been lost.
28507	Rebuild the context.</strong> Put back the fixed instructions you need, the tool definitions, the current environment and the candidate compaction state; avoid duplication, and avoid raising permissions by mistake.
21163	Switch conditionally.</strong> Switch only while the original snapshot version is still valid, and save the index of the old records together with the compaction version. A failed compaction must not empty a valid history.
27200	These seven steps do not mean that every chat tool needs a complex database. A short task may not compact at all; a lightweight implementation can use a write-once new file and an atomic rename; stronger version conditions are needed only once concurrency and long-running tasks are involved.
27821	Follow-up: when is the best moment to trigger compaction?
24757	Weigh the remaining window, the expected next tool result, the stage boundaries, how relevant the history still is, and what the cache gains or loses.

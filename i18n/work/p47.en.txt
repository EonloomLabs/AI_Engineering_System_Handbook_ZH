7324	D.10｜Six follow-up questions and directions for complete answers
28712	Question: why not reread every file after compaction?</strong> Rereading restores some current facts at high cost, but not past external effects absent from source. Recover evidence and unresolved operations relevant to the next step first; current code is not complete history.
28716	Question: can a stronger model find summary errors automatically?</strong> Only if summaries or other evidence reveal contradictions. Consistent "failed" input cannot reliably reveal changed files to any model. Capability cannot replace missing observations.
28715	Question: do retries on every tool improve reliability?</strong> Distinguish read-only idempotent operations from side effects; timeouts do not prove nonexecution. Operation semantics, idempotency keys or result queries matter; retry counts do not create reliability.
28714	Question: can passing tests justify ignoring duplicate actions?</strong> No. Repeated edits may be harmless by chance or cause extra external effects, confused history or future risks. Evaluate code outcomes and side effects separately; one green state cannot cover everything.
28711	Question: why not let a Reviewer decide what unknown means?</strong> It also needs real evidence. Reviewers can propose checks but gain no external facts through role names. Another model does not remove information gaps.
28713	Question: which layer actually improves?</strong> Execution state and context handoff are the main repair; selective loading and compaction timing are secondary. No evidence requires changed model parameters. Locating measures by layer avoids expensive irrelevant optimization.

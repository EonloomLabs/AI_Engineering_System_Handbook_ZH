The limits of use for this index

This book gives a systematic account of the main mechanisms needed from language models through to agent engineering, together with the prerequisites, the core derivations and the matching limits. It cannot replace a complete specialist textbook on linear algebra, probability theory or distributed systems, and it does not cover every published paper. Where product behavior has not been measured or made public, it avoids unproven assertions.

Reading guide and appendix

References and scope of use

Reading toolabout 3.3k characters

The sources below support mechanisms within the scope stated in the text. Chinese explanations, derivations and examples are provided locally; links supply provenance rather than required external reading steps.

How to distinguish three kinds of material

Research and official documentation describe public facts; independent hand calculations and constructed cases explain mechanisms; program run records correspond to specified data and assertions. Teaching numbers do not represent commercial model parameters, and deterministic tests do not represent real agent success rates. Product and protocol descriptions should match the listed versions.
